# Website Audit Checklist

## Strategy
- Who is the page for?
- What action should they take?
- Is the offer obvious within 5 seconds?

## Visuals
- Strong hero composition
- Clear typography hierarchy
- Consistent spacing
- Real proof/photos where possible
- Premium but not cluttered

## UX
- Navigation is obvious
- CTA repeats naturally
- Service/process sections reduce uncertainty
- Contact friction is low

## Motion
- Motion supports comprehension
- No nausea-inducing scroll effects
- Reduced-motion fallback exists

## 3D
- 3D has a purpose
- Lazy-loaded
- Fallback image exists
- Mobile safe

## Performance
- Compressed images
- Fonts optimized
- Heavy scripts deferred
- Lighthouse checked

## Accessibility
- Contrast passes
- Keyboard nav works
- Focus states visible
- Forms labelled
- Semantic landmarks used
