# SEO Roadmap — Turbo Contracting and Mining Services
**Created:** 2026-06-14

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation (Week 1–2)

These are blocking items. Nothing else will perform well without these.

- [ ] **Add phone number to site** — header and footer. Update LocalBusiness schema. Most critical conversion and local pack signal.
- [ ] **Verify Google Search Console** — DNS TXT record via Cloudflare dashboard. Submit sitemap.xml.
- [ ] **Claim Google Business Profile** — use info@turbocontracting.ca. Set category to General Contractor. Add services, photos, description.
- [ ] **Update page title tags** — apply recommendations from `SEO/metadata/titles.csv`
- [ ] **Update meta descriptions** — apply recommendations from `SEO/metadata/descriptions.csv`

---

## Phase 2 — Schema & Structure (Week 2–3)

- [ ] **Embed FAQPage schema** on homepage or contact page (use `SEO/schema/faq.json`)
- [ ] **Create /404.html** — custom Cloudflare Pages 404 with nav + contact link
- [ ] **Add Service schema per page** — embed individual service schemas from `SEO/schema/services.json` on the services page
- [ ] **Add H1 keyword optimization on services page** — e.g., "Industrial Millwright Services Northern Ontario" vs. generic "Industrial Millwright & Mill Maintenance"

---

## Phase 3 — Local Authority (Month 1–2)

- [ ] **Build 5–10 core citations** — see `SEO/checklists/local-seo-checklist.md`
- [ ] **Start GBP post cadence** — 1–2 posts per month using `SEO/content/gbp-posts.md` templates
- [ ] **Request first 3 Google reviews** — from past clients. Direct link from GBP dashboard.
- [ ] **Add "Service Areas" section to homepage** — list key cities: Timmins, Sudbury, Thunder Bay, Kirkland Lake, Cochrane, Sault Ste. Marie

---

## Phase 4 — Content Expansion (Month 2–4)

- [ ] **City landing pages** — "Mining Contractor Timmins", "Millwright Sudbury" etc. — target geo+service combos
- [ ] **FAQ section on contact page** — 6 Q&As already written in `SEO/schema/faq.json`
- [ ] **Case studies or project writeups** — even 1–2 paragraphs per project type builds unique content
- [ ] **Monthly blog/news** — "What to expect during a plant shutdown", "Why laser alignment matters" — informational content for long-tail

---

## Phase 5 — Ongoing (Monthly)

- [ ] Run monthly SEO review using `.ai/workflows/monthly-seo-workflow.md`
- [ ] Track keyword rankings for top 10 queries in Search Console
- [ ] Publish 1 GBP post per month
- [ ] Add 1–2 new reviews per quarter
- [ ] Refresh any page with declining CTR (update title/description)
