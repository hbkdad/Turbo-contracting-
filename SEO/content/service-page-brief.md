# Service Page Content Brief — Turbo Contracting

Use this brief when adding new service pages or expanding existing sections.

## Target audience
Mine site managers, exploration project coordinators, plant maintenance supervisors,
construction project managers — across Northern Ontario.
Decision makers who need a contractor they can trust on short notice.

## Voice & tone
- Direct, confident, no fluff
- Tradespeople talking to industrial decision-makers
- Measurable differentiators (certified, in-house, fast mobilization)
- Zero jargon inflation — say what the work is

## Page structure (per service)
1. **Kicker label** — Service category
2. **H1** — Service name (keyword-rich, e.g. "Industrial Sandblasting & Painting Northern Ontario")
3. **Intro paragraph** — What the service is, who needs it, why Turbo does it right (2–3 sentences)
4. **Detail paragraph** — How we approach the work, what makes it different (1–2 sentences)
5. **Capability list** — 5–8 bullet points of specific deliverables
6. **Sidebar CTA card** — "Ready to discuss [service]?" → Request a Quote button
7. **Optional: photo or B/A comparison**

## Title tag formula
[Service] [Location] | Turbo Contracting
Max 60 chars. Include "Northern Ontario" or a city name.

## Description formula
[What we do] in [location]. [Key capability or differentiator]. [CTA phrase].
Max 155 chars.

## Internal linking
Every service section → contact.html (CTA button)
Every service section → services.html (if standalone page)
Homepage → services.html (main nav + hero CTA)
