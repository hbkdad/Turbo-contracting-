Our Story

One contractor. One standard of work.

Turbo Contracting and Mining Services was built on a simple premise: industrial and mining clients deserve a contractor who shows up prepared, does the work correctly the first time, and answers the phone when something needs attention.

We're based in Northern Ontario and we work here because this is the region we know. Mining operations, exploration programs, pulp mills, construction sites, utility infrastructure: we understand the demands these environments put on people and equipment, and we understand what it takes to work safely and properly inside them.

From mill maintenance and welding to road building, painting, and confined space entry at active mine sites. Turbo Contracting and Mining Services carries the certifications and field experience to execute a wide scope of mining and industrial work. Every trade we deploy is our own. We don't broker labour or pass scope to subcontractors. When you hire us, you get our tradespeople, our quality standards, and direct accountability from the owner.

Certifications & Safety

Certified for the sites we work on.

Confined Space

Entry and attendant certification for permit-required confined spaces. We provide both the entry worker and the stand-by watch, with atmospheric monitoring and rescue equipment on site.

Working at Heights

Certified for elevated work with proper fall protection systems, rescue planning, and equipment in place before anyone leaves the ground.

Hoisting & Rigging

Certified riggers for critical lifts, heavy equipment picks, and underground hoisting work. Lift planning, hardware inspection, and load control done right.

How We Work

From first call to final sign-off.

We run a tight process. Here's what working with Turbo Contracting and Mining Services looks like from start to finish.

01

Project Inquiry

Tell us the scope: what's failing, what needs installing, what your shutdown requires, or what your site needs built. We'll ask the right questions before we quote it.

02

Site Assessment

For complex or unfamiliar scopes, we assess the site before pricing. Better information produces better quotes and fewer surprises in the field.

03

Honest Quote

You get a straight number and a clear scope. We don't low-ball to win jobs and change-order our way to margin. The quote reflects the work.

04

Our tradespeople show up prepared, with the tools and parts needed. Work proceeds to plan, with daily communication until the job is done and verified.
